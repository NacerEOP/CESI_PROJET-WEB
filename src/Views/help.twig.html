🔍 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top navigation. Fill in your details including your email, password, and personal information. You'll receive a confirmation email to activate your account.

How do I apply for an internship?

Browse internships on the "Browse" page, click on an internship that interests you, and click "Apply Now". Make sure your CV is uploaded in your profile.

How do I upload my CV?

Go to your profile page, click on "Upload CV", and select your CV file (PDF, DOC, or DOCX format, max 5MB).

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. You'll receive instructions to reset your password.

Why can't I see certain internships?

Some internships may be restricted to specific user roles (students, admins, pilots). Make sure you're logged in with the appropriate account type.

How do I contact a company?

View the company details on the browse page or companies page. Contact information is provided there.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

Page not loading properly

Try refreshing the page (F5 or Ctrl+R). Clear your browser cache and cookies. Make sure you're using a modern browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.

Login issues

Check that your email and password are correct. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link. Ensure your account is activated via email.

Application submission failed

Ensure all required fields are filled. Check your internet connection. If the problem persists, try again later or contact support.

CV upload failed

Make sure your file is in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format and under 5MB. Check your internet connection and try again.

Search not working

Try using different keywords or filters. Clear any applied filters and search again.
